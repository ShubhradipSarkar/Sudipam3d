FR. 0014 / ABOUT

The Craft Behind the Movement

I am a Senior 3D Animator based in Santipur, West Bengal, with over a decade of hands-on production experience across leading animation studios in India.

My work spans character animation, acting and body mechanics, product visualization, medical animation, and long-form episodic television. At Unicorn Animation Studio I contributed to episodic and product animations over seven years. Before that, I brought characters to life for productions like Motu Patlu and Rudra at Green Beetle.

My foundation was built through focused character work at Frame Picture Studio and Applita Interactive. Today, as an independent animator, I partner with studios and direct clients on projects that demand precision, creative integrity, and respect for storytelling through motion — using Autodesk Maya, supported by Adobe Premiere Pro and Photoshop for finishing.
